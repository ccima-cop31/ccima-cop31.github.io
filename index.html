Biodiversity · Carbon · Ecosystem services

GEMS

Connecting biodiversity restoration with carbon markets

Global Ecosystem Model Services uses biodiversity–ecosystem service relationships to inform carbon assessment, restoration planning and climate adaptation.

  • Explore how biodiversity relates to carbon storage and other ecosystem services.
  • Assess restoration opportunities under changing environmental conditions.
  • Connect ecological evidence with monitoring and financing discussions.

Microbiomes · Modelling · Agriculture

DigiMic

Designing microbial solutions for resilient agriculture

DigiMic is our developing research and modelling toolkit for understanding microbial communities and guiding the design and testing of useful combinations.

  • Represent resource use, microbial interactions and environmental responses.
  • Prioritise candidate communities and informative experiments.
  • Develop agricultural applications, including the proposed DigiMic-P phosphorus biofertiliser.

Agriculture in a coastal landscape

Coastal landscapes connect agriculture, water and biodiversity. DigiMic's agricultural development work explores how model-guided microbial design could support resilient crop systems under changing conditions.

From a practical challenge
to a jointly designed pilot.

We seek agricultural businesses, research institutions and public-sector partners to co-develop and fund defined pilots, connecting modelling and experiments with staged agricultural validation and agreed success measures. Explore DigiMic.
